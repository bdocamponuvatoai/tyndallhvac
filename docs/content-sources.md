## Deliberate omissions

The current source website has inconsistent review figures, experience totals, contact emails, and hours. Its footer address appears to be placeholder content. This site omits the street address, review counts, testimonials, ratings, hours, guarantee claims, price offers and specific response-time promises. It uses the Gmail address in the main contact section and the phone corroborated above. The owner should confirm production contact details before public launch.

Service-area cities are selected from the official website’s service-area navigation. The directory is not an exhaustive promise of availability; the interface asks visitors to confirm their address and scheduling directly.

All new wording is original editorial copy. No testimonials have been invented. All photos are AI-generated illustrations, not portfolio evidence. The simple Tyndall mark is a proposed design treatment, not a recovered official logo. No certification logos or manufacturer partnerships have been invented or implied.

## Scope

A static website with a client-side inquiry planner that prepares an email draft. It is not connected to live scheduling, CRM, email sending, analytics, customer accounts, payment processing, or a service dispatch system. It is not public until the owner approves it (search engines are blocked until then).

Where these facts appear in code: `src/config/site.ts` (business details), `src/content/` (services, FAQs, service areas, home copy).
